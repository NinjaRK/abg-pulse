How source health works

Each source receives a rolling operating baseline. Explicit fetch errors, invalid schemas, unexpected volume drops and registry drift are surfaced. A legitimate day with no filings is allowed only where the source explicitly supports empty results.

Plain-English definitions

Every important label should be understandable without a methodology degree.

Must Know

Material enough to affect strategy, reputation, regulation, leadership, capital or a major business—and backed by a Tier-0 source or two independent source origins.

Evidence rule

A disputed or material single-source claim cannot remain Must Know. It is held in Watch until verified.

Watch

Potentially important, evolving or disputed. Worth monitoring, but not yet strong enough for decisive action.

Evidence rule

Unresolved contradictions, credible single-source reports and emerging narrative risk belong here.

Media Tone

Language used by observed publishers about the entity or event. It is not the same as broad public sentiment.

What it cannot claim

It cannot represent people who did not publish, nor closed social platforms that are not licensed and connected.

Observed Public Sentiment

Tone in the public-conversation channels actually observed, shown with sample size and confidence.

What “observed” protects against

It prevents a narrow sample from being presented as the opinion of the whole public.
